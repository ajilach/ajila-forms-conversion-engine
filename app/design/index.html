How reuse works: the markup and component classes (.panel, .btn, .steps, .thumbs, .chip …) are identical in every preview. A theme is just a redefinition of CSS custom properties (--accent, --surface, --radius, --shadow …) plus a few signature overrides. Picking a winner means porting these tokens into the app's app/assets/styles.css token block.
